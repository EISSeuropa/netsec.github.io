Practical information

Plan your visit

Accommodation

Look for accommodation in town. Any of the neighbourhoods below puts you within a short ride of Stockholm University. Södermalm tends to be the most affordable and has the widest choice of hostels.

  • Södermalm

    Cheaper end of the centre, plenty of hostels.

    • Hornstull
    • Zinkensdamm
    • Mariatorget
    • Slussen

  • Gamla stan

    Old Town, on its own island in the centre.

    • Gamla stan

  • T-Centralen

    Stockholm City, the main interchange.

    • T-Centralen

  • Östermalm

    Quieter, slightly upscale.

    • Östermalmstorg

  • Stadion

    Near the 1912 Olympic Stadium.

    • Stadion

Getting around

Stockholm's red metro line (T13) runs from the far south to the far north of the city. All the recommended neighbourhoods above sit on it, so the ride to Stockholm University is short and direct.

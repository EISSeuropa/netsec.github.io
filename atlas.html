The NetSec Atlas

Prototype

A live map of the network behind Networking European Security Knowledge. Two lenses over the same people: the Working Groups they sit in, and the research themes they work on (the map of the field). Overlay who shared a conference panel at ESSC 2026, or who is offering and seeking mentorship. Co-authorship joins the map on its own as the Action’s publications are added. People between two hubs are the bridges of the Action. Hover a face or dot for who it is, click through to their directory profile where one exists. Everything is generated from the same data that drives the Working Groups page and the Directory.

This website is based upon work from COST Action NetSec: Networking European Security Knowledge, supported by COST (European Cooperation in Science and Technology).

COST (European Cooperation in Science and Technology) is a funding agency for research and innovation networks. Our Actions help connect research initiatives across Europe and enable scientists to grow their ideas by sharing them with their peers. This boosts their research, career and innovation.

The NetSec Atlas

Prototype

A live map of the network behind Networking European Security Knowledge. Two lenses over the same people: the Working Groups they sit in, and the research themes they work on (the map of the field). Overlay who shared a conference panel at ESSC 2026, or who is offering and seeking mentorship. People between two hubs are the bridges of the Action. Hover a face or dot for who it is, click through to their directory profile where one exists. Everything is generated from the same data that drives the Working Groups page and the Directory.

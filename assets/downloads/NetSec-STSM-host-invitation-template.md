# STSM host invitation-letter template

A skeleton for NetSec members whose institution hosts a Short-Term
Scientific Mission (STSM) visitor. Adapt the wording to your
institution's conventions and put the final letter on institutional
letterhead. Replace every bracketed placeholder before sending. The
visitor attaches the letter to their application on e-COST.

---

[Institution letterhead]

[Date]

Dear [Visitor name],

On behalf of [Institution], I am pleased to invite you to undertake a
Short-Term Scientific Mission under COST Action CA24154 (NetSec) at
our institution.

We understand that the purpose of your visit is [Purpose], and that
the visit is planned for [Dates]. During this period you will be
hosted by [Host name], who will act as your host and point of contact.
We will provide you with access to the facilities and support
reasonably required to carry out the work described above.

We look forward to welcoming you and to a productive collaboration.

Yours sincerely,

[Host name]
[Position]
[Institution]
[Contact details]

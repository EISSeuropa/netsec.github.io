Mr Mohamed El Yamine Atrouche

Mohamed El Yamine Atrouche is a nuclear security, emerging and disruptive technologies researcher. He currently serves as a project researcher with the British American Security Information Council's Emerging Voices Network in the second iteration of the Assembling Peace through Dialogue Project and as a working group chair of the nuclear justice working group for Taking Stock of the First Five Years of the TPNW Policy Cycle. He is also a research fellow in the CTBTO Research Fellowship 2026.

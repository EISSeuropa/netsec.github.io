PhD candidate Linnaeus University Sweden EU enlargement, Western Balkans, European integration

Ms Anna Seliverstova

I am a PhD candidate in Political Science at Linnaeus University, Sweden. My research focuses on EU enlargement, democratic backsliding, and the politics of reform in the Western Balkans, with a particular focus on Serbia. More broadly, I am interested in EU external governance, conditionality, civil society, and how candidate countries navigate the accession process.

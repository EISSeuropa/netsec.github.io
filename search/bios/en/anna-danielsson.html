Prof. Anna Danielsson

Professor Anna Danielsson's research is situated at the nexus of war studies, the sociology of war, international political sociology, social theory, critical peacebuilding studies, and the history and sociology of knowledge. Thematically, she currently conducts research in two main areas. First, Anna works on issues pertaining to urban security and urban war, urban warfare, and urbicide. Second, she conducts research on the epistemics of international interventions and military operations – with a specific focus on urban peacebuilding and the entangled production of knowledge and space in urban peacebuilding interventions.

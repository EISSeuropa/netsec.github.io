Dr Laurențiu Pleşca

Laurențiu Pleșca holds a PhD in Political Science and is a Program Officer at the German Marshall Fund's Black Sea Trust for Regional Cooperation. He is also a researcher at the Romanian Center for Russian Studies. His research covers Moldova's European integration, the Transnistrian conflict, Russian hybrid warfare and disinformation, EU enlargement, and security in the Black Sea region.

Researcher ETH Zürich, Center for Security Studies Switzerland Deterrence, European Security, Alliance politics, NATO

Mr Immanuel Rummel

I am an early-career researcher working on issues in German and European security. I am particularly interested in Germany’s and Europe’s roles in a world of US retrenchment and geopolitical competition. My latest research project uses a network perspective to examine how non-nuclear powers restructure their deterrence when their nuclear patron retrenches.

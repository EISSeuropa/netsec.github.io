PhD Candidate University of Exeter United Kingdom Critical Security, Settler-Colonial Studies, Surveillance

Mr Khalil Talhaoui

PhD candidate in Security, Conflict, and Human Rights at University of Exeter. I hold a Master in War Studies at the Swedish Defence University and a Master in Critical Criminology and Penitentiary Law at the University of Barcelona. My research is situated at the nexus of Critical Security Studies, Queer Studies, Settler-Colonial Studies and Surveillance Studies with a particular focus on settler colonial practices of (in)security and surveillance.

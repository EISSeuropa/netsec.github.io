Prof. Kenan Hodžić

Dr. Kenan Hodžić is an Assistant Professor at the Faculty of Criminal Justice and Security Studies, University of Sarajevo, where he teaches courses including Fundamentals of Security, Crisis Management, and Critical Infrastructure Protection. His research focuses on contemporary national security, energy security, and cross-domain physical and IT/OT security convergence. Dr. Hodžić has extensive international project experience, serving as a national expert on EU-funded initiatives across South East and Eastern Europe, Central Asia, and the Middle East, as well as contributing as a research assistant on NATO-supported projects. He actively leads advanced capacity-building programs and specialized academic schools dedicated to enhancing critical infrastructure protection.

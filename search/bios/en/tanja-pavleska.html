Dr Tanja Pavleska

Tanja Pavleska is a Senior Researcher at the Laboratory for Open Systems and Networks, Jožef Stefan Institute. She holds a degree in Automation and Systems Engineering and a PhD in Advanced Internet Technologies, with emphasis on user behavior in digital environments. Her research interests and expertise include the architecture design, security, and trustworthiness of digital infrastructures, information and data governance, digital policies for trust and identity management, artificial intelligence for society and society for artificial intelligence, with a focus on cybersecurity and safety. She has participated in numerous European R&I projects focused on the digital transformation of the public sector and the society in general, in view of achieving cross-border interoperability, governance, and secure service provision.

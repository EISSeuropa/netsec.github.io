Mr Marco Cifra

Marco Cifra is a Ph.D. student in Defence and Security Studies at the Centre for Higher Defence Studies (CASD) in Rome, specializing in international law with a focus on space law, international humanitarian law, and the regulation of artificial intelligence in military contexts. His research examines the legal implications of AI-driven decision support systems in warfare, with particular attention to targeting processes, the principles of proportionality and precaution, and the concept of meaningful human control.

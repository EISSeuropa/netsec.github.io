Dr Liliana Pasquale

Liliana Pasquale received her Ph.D. degree from Politecnico di Milano, Italy, in 2011. She is currently an Associate Professor at University College Dublin (UCD), Ireland. She is the founder and director of the MSc in Cybersecurity at UCD. Her research interests include requirements engineering and adaptive systems, with a focus on security and digital forensics. She will be the General Co-chair of ICSE 2027 and has served as PC Co-Chair of SEAMS 2024 and REFSQ 2020. She has served in the program and organising committee for prestigious software engineering conferences, such as ICSE, FSE, ASE, RE, and SEAMS. She is an Associate Editor-in-Chief of IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering, the Department Editor of IEEE Security & Privacy Magazine, and Associate Editor of the Requirements Engineering journal.

Lecturer Yildiz Technical University Türkiye WG2 Middle East Studies, International Relations, Foreign Policy, Securiy Studies, Diplomacy, Turkish Foreign Policy

Dr Mehmet Babacan

Mehmet BABACAN was awarded his PhD degree in Middle East Studies from the Department of International Relations at Bursa Uludag University. With numerous national and international publications in the fields of international politics, Turkish foreign policy, Middle East Studies and Jerusalem and Palestinian studies, Babacan currently serves at Yıldız Technical University. Babacan, who has served as an expert and researcher on numerous YOK (Turkish Higher Education Council) and EU (European Union) projects, is married, has three children and speaks Turkish, English, Arabic.

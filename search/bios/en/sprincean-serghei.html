Serghei SPRINCEAN ORCID: https://orcid.org/0000-0002-7411-9958, Scopus Author ID: 58172233200, WoS ID: V-3296-2017, https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=z4vnBmoAAAAJ I activate as scientific secretary and researcher of the Institute of Legal, Political and Sociological Research of the Moldova State University. I am author of over 330 scientific publications on the specific of assuring human safety and security, assessment and evaluation of public policies, the role of the interests and pressure groups and of the civil society in decision-making process. I am coordinating the international project "Camps and Camp-like Settings in Urban Environments: Bridging Humanitarian and Development Activities", funded by Swiss Network for International Studies (SNIS), hosting institution: Fachhochschule Nordwestschweiz (FHNW), international Consortium between University of Applied Sciences Northwestern Switzerland, Moldova State University and University of Jordan (2025 - 2027) - as national coordinator. As well I participated in such projects as 20.70086.13/COV "Mitigation of the negative impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on the functioning of state power institutions in the Republic of Moldova (FUNCOV)" (2020-2021 also as coordinator), 20.80009.1606.05 "Quality of justice and respect for human rights in the Republic of Moldova: interdisciplinary research in the context of the implementation of the Association Agreement Republic of Moldova - European Union" (2020-2023), "The development of legal framework of Republic of Moldova in the context of the necessities in security and in assuring European path” (2015-2019), “Interaction between state and civil society in Republic of Moldova in the context of deepening of democratic reforms” (2011-2014).

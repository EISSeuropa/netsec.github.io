Prof. Olivier Schmitt

I am professor and head of research at the Institute for Military Operations at the Royal Danish Defence College. I am also a senior non-resident associate fellow at the NATO Defence College, and an associate at RAND Europe. I am currently an associate editor of the European Journal of International Security and a board member of the Journal of Strategic Studies and of the Review of International Studies.

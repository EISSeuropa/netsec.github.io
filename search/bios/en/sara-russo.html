Ms Sara Russo

Sara Russo is a Ph.D. candidate in Defence and Security Studies at the Centre for Higher Defence Studies (CASD) and the University of Turin, where her research focuses on cognitive warfare, strategic communication, influence dynamics, leadership, and organizational resilience. Her work explores how influence operates across social, organizational, and technological systems, with particular attention to the evolving challenges posed by hybrid threats, information environments, and emerging forms of conflict.

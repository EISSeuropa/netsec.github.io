Dr Mir Hassan

Mir Hassan is an Associate Professor in Artificial Intelligence & Trustworthy AI and a Senior Researcher at Mykolas Romeris University, Lithuania. He also collaborates with researchers at the University of Trento, Italy. His research focuses on federated learning, distributed intelligence, and trustworthy AI, including communication-efficient learning and model evaluation across participants with heterogeneous data.

His work examines how institutions and devices can train machine learning models collaboratively while retaining their data locally. This technical background informs his interest in European security questions concerning sensitive data, technological dependence, and the reliability of AI systems. He is particularly interested in examining the practical capabilities and limitations of distributed AI through collaboration with security scholars and practitioners.

Within NetSec, he seeks to contribute to interdisciplinary knowledge exchange, accessible explanations of AI methods, and research training. He welcomes collaboration on evaluating AI systems, understanding the privacy limitations of collaborative learning, and developing reproducible research across disciplinary boundaries.

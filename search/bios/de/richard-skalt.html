Advocacy Manager, Cybersecurity Office TÜV SÜD Germany International Security, Cybersecurity Regulation, AI Regulation

Mr Richard Skalt

Richard Skalt is Advocacy Manager at TÜV SÜD’s Cybersecurity Office. Previously he worked he was the Manager of ESMT Berlin’s Digital Society Institute, and held various positions at the German Council on Foreign Relations (DGAP), the University of Munich and Check Point Software Technologies. Richard is a political scientist by training with expertise in cybersecurity, tech policy, and research on critical infrastructure protection and AI. He graduated from the Double Degree Master Program in Political Science at Stockholm University (SU) and Ludwig Maximilian University (LMU) Munich.

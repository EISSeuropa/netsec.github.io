Mr Emmanouil Daskalakis

Manolis Daskalakis is a Fire Officer in the Hellenic Fire Service, Greece, with professional experience in operational response, crisis management, civil protection and security. He holds a BSc in Geology from Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, an MBA in Oil, Gas and Energy Management from the University of Nicosia, and an MSc in Environmental and Disaster and Crisis Management from the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens. He has also completed vocational training in Security Management of Persons and Infrastructure at the 2nd Public Vocational Training Institute of Heraklion.

He is currently a PhD candidate at the Hellenic Army Academy, conducting research on information flow and interagency coordination for achieving security against asymmetric threats. In parallel, he is a postgraduate student in the MSc programme in Energy Systems at the Hellenic Mediterranean University.

His professional background includes operational and staff positions within the Hellenic Fire Service, teaching and training activities at the Hellenic Fire Academy, and previous experience in the private security sector. He has also worked as a security analyst and journalist, contributing articles on national and international security, energy, geopolitics and emergency management to various media outlets.

His research and professional interests focus on national and European security, hybrid threats, civil protection, disaster risk reduction, critical infrastructure protection, energy security and interagency cooperation. He is particularly interested in strengthening societal resilience and developing integrated approaches to security through cooperation between emergency response services, armed forces, law enforcement agencies and critical infrastructure operators.

His long-term goal is to combine expertise in law, security, and artificial intelligence to contribute to innovation in both the legal and security sectors.

Committed to lifelong learning, he regularly participates in international training courses, webinars, conferences, and professional development programs offered by leading academic institutions, international organizations, and policy think tanks. Through these activities, he continues to strengthen his analytical, technical, and interdisciplinary skills while building a global professional network.

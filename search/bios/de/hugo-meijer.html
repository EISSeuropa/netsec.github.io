Dr Hugo Meijer

Hugo Meijer is CNRS Research Fellow at the Center for International Studies (CERI) at Sciences Po, Paris. He is also the Founding and Honorary Director of the European Initiative for Security Studies, the largest European network of security studies scholars. Italian, French and Dutch (trinational), his research focuses on the deep history of war and peace and long-term processes of biocultural evolution. Recent and forthcoming publications include: The Origins of War and Peace in the Human Species (Cambridge University Press, 2026); with Richard Wrangham, “The Evolutionary Roots of War and Peace,” Journal of Strategic Studies, 48(6-7) (2026), pp. 1134–1222; “Janus faced: The Co-Evolution of War and Peace in the Human Species,” Evolutionary Anthropology 33/3 (2024), pp. 1-14; “The Origins of War: A Global Archaeological Review,” Human Nature, 35(3) (2024), pp. 225–288. For more details on his profile, see: https://www.hugomeijer.com.

Dr Tolga Cikrikci

Dr Tolga Cikrikci is an Assistant Professor of International Relations at Giresun University, Türkiye. His research focuses on international law, human rights, global governance and international security.

His recent work examines the legal and normative dimensions of the use of force, the relationship between international law and security, and the resilience of international organisations under changing political conditions. He is particularly interested in how international institutions respond to geopolitical challenges and how international legal norms are interpreted and applied during international crises.

His broader research interests include human security, discrimination and the role of international organisations in global governance. He also teaches courses on international law, international human rights law and international organisations.

Within NetSec, he is interested in interdisciplinary academic collaboration, knowledge exchange and strengthening the relationship between academic research and policy discussions on contemporary security challenges.

Assistant Professor Giresun University Türkiye International Law, Human Security, Global Governance, International Organizations, Human Rights

Dr Tolga Çıkrıkçı

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of International Relations at Giresun University, Türkiye, where I teach undergraduate and graduate courses in Public International Law, International Human Rights Law, International Organizations, and Private International Law. I hold a PhD in International Relations, and my academic work focuses on the intersection of international law and international politics.

My research interests include international law, human rights, human security, global governance, international organizations, and contemporary security challenges. In my recent work, I have examined topics such as vaccine hesitancy and human security, discrimination and intolerance in the European Union, global justice, and the role of international law in responding to armed conflicts and international crises.

Alongside my research and teaching, I regularly present my work at national and international conferences and serve as a reviewer for academic journals in the fields of international relations and international law. I also participate in international research collaborations and contribute to academic quality assurance and accreditation processes.

Through my research, teaching, and international engagement, I aim to contribute to a deeper understanding of contemporary global challenges and the evolving role of international law and global governance in addressing them.

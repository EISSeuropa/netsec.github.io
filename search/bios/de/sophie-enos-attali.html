Associate Professor Catholic University of Paris France Security politices, Nordic countries, military neutrality, small states, Nordic-Baltic space

Dr Sophie Enos-Attali

After a PhD on the security policies of some European "neutral" countries, I've been working for several years both on the security policies of Nordic countries and on the security policies of European "neutral" countries in the framework of the war in Ukraine. I teach International Relations and Political Science at the Catholic University of Paris and I am an associate researcher at Centre Thucydide (Université Panthéon-Assas) and editorial coordinator of L'Année des relations internationales, a yearly French-speaking journal dedicated to International Relations.

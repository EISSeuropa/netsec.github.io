Dr Roie Yellinek

Dr. Roie Yellinek is Co-Director of Geopolitics Lab (G-Lab), a geopolitical consultancy, and geopolitical advisor to the Israeli National AI Directorate. He previously served as a security and strategic consultant and researcher for the Israeli government; as a research associate at the Begin-Sadat Center for Strategic Studies (BESA); as a non-resident scholar at the Middle East Institute (MEI) in Washington, D.C.; and has published extensively on China–Middle East relations, regional security, and great-power competition.

Prof. Islam Jusufi

Islam Jusufi is a political science researcher and academic who is based in Skopje, North Macedonia. He holds an Associate Professorship in political science, awarded by Tirana University, Albania. Previously, he was a lecturer and department head at Epoka University, Albania. His research focuses on European and Balkan governance and security.

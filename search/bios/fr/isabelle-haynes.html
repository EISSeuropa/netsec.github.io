Ms Isabelle Haynes

Isabelle Haynes is a PhD candidate in the Area Studies program at the Institute of International Studies, Faculty of Social Sciences, Charles University. She is a Junior Research at the Peace Research Centre Prague and the PhD student on the ERC-project MICROCODE. She holds an MSc in Migration Studies and an MPhil in International Relations from the University of Oxford, as well as a BA in Archaeology and Classics from University College London. Isabelle has professional experience across the public, private, and academic sectors, including roles at UN Tourism, the UK Ministry of Defence, the Nuffield Centre for Experimental Social Science, and SouthWest Archaeology.

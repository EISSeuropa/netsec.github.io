# Per-member Open Graph cards

Each directory member has a personal social-share image at
`assets/og/people/<slug>.png` (1200×630). When a member's `/people/<slug>`
profile is shared on Bluesky, LinkedIn, Slack, etc., it unfurls as **that
person** (headshot, name, role, working-group and mentorship pills, country
flag, NetSec branding) rather than the generic site card. Issue #1023.

You never touch these by hand. Member data flows in and
`scripts/build-og-cards.py` draws the cards, which are **not committed**. The
Pages deploy renders all 84 on its way to publishing, in about seventy seconds
(#1716). `assets/og/people/` is gitignored.

## The chain

```
Google Form → Google Sheet → sync-bios.yml (weekly) → data/bios.json
                                          │
                                          ▼  scripts/build-og-cards.py
                              data/bios.json
                                          │
                                          ▼  pages-deploy.yml, every deploy
                                 scripts/build-og-cards.py
                                          │
                                          ▼
                              assets/og/people/<slug>.png  (gitignored)
```

`build-profile-pages.py` names `{SITE}/assets/og/people/<slug>.png` as each
profile's `og:image` unconditionally, since the builder above draws a card for
every member carrying an id. It used to test the file on disk, which would now
point all 252 pages at the generic card.

## What's on the card, and what triggers a re-render

A card is keyed by a hash of exactly these fields (`card_hash` in the script):

- name, position, affiliation
- country (and its flag, bundled under `assets/og/flags/`)
- headshot: the photo path **and** the photo's source-image SHA-256
- working-group pills (WG1–WG4, with a lead / co-lead marker)
- mentor / mentee
- STSM hosting

Change any of those and **that member's card re-renders**. Research keywords and
themes are deliberately **not** on the card, so editing only those does not
re-render it.

## Lifecycle

- **A member joins** — the weekly bios sync writes them into `data/bios.json`;
  the builder renders their new card and the profile build points their
  `og:image` at it.
- **A member edits their tags** — a change to their working group, mentorship,
  STSM hosting, role, affiliation, country, or headshot flips the hash, so
  **only their card** is rebuilt.
- **A member is removed** — the builder prunes the manifest entry and deletes
  the orphan PNG.

## Why it stays churn-free

`assets/og/people/_manifest.json` stores the input-hash per member, and the
builder **re-renders only the members whose hash changed or whose PNG is
missing**. That is a local convenience now: edit one member and re-run, and one
card is drawn rather than 84. A clean checkout has no manifest, which is why the
deploy renders the full set every time. PNGs are not byte-reproducible across
Chrome and font versions, so a hash of the inputs is the freshness test rather
than a pixel diff.

## Rendering

Cards are captured with headless Google Chrome over the **DevTools Protocol**
(`Page.captureScreenshot` with an exact 1200×630 clip), not the `--screenshot`
CLI flag — on macOS the CLI subtracts the window chrome and clips the footer, so
CDP gives the same exact size on macOS and the Linux CI runner. The render needs
Chrome/Chromium.

## Guard rails and manual use

There is no drift gate any more. A gate exists to prove a committed artefact is
current, and there is no committed artefact, so `--check` went with the cards.
Three things stand in its place:

- `pages-deploy.yml` counts the rendered cards against the members in
  `data/bios.json` and **refuses to publish** if the two disagree. Under-rendering
  is the failure that matters, because the profile pages name the card URL
  without testing it.
- `test_every_profile_page_gets_a_card` asserts the two predicates agree, so the
  two builders cannot drift apart on who gets one.
- The social workflows render the spotlight member's single card before posting
  (`--only`), since the tree they check out has none. That step is
  `continue-on-error`, so a Chrome failure posts the generic card rather than
  nothing.

`sync-bios.yml` still runs `--ensure-flags` weekly and commits any new country
flag. A flag is an input, not a derived card.

Manual commands (run from the repo root):

```bash
python3 scripts/build-og-cards.py            # render the stale cards + manifest
python3 scripts/build-og-cards.py --only SLUG # render one member's card
python3 scripts/build-og-cards.py --force     # re-render everything
```

The separate **directory** card (`assets/images/og-image-people.png`, the
`/people.html` share image) is a different asset, generated by
`scripts/build-directory-og.py`; it is not per-member. The general card
(`assets/images/og-image.png`, every other page) is generated the same way by
`scripts/build-general-og.py`.

## Event-page cards

Each page that an event in `data/events.json` names as its `url` has its own
card in each locale, at `assets/og/events/<page>.jpg`, `<page>.fr.jpg` and
`<page>.de.jpg` (#2185): today `essc-2026`, `essc-2027`,
`policy-workshop-2026`, `policy-workshop-2027` and `summer-school`, so fifteen
cards. A page with more than one event (the Training School page holds the
2026 and 2027 editions) shows the latest.

Every word on a card comes from what the event listings already show in that
locale:

- the title from `cardTitle`, or for a policy workshop from `cardSubtitle`
  (its `cardTitle` is only "NetSec Policy Workshop", which the label above
  the title already says);
- the date from `displayDate`;
- the institution from the bold part of the event's map-pin line (`meta`,
  icon `pin`), plus a faculty named right after it;
- the city and country from `cardLocation`.

The event-type label, "COST Action CA24154" and the EU funding line are fixed
strings in `_event_cards.TYPE_LABEL` and `build-event-og.py`. The funding line
is the EU's own wording of its emblem statement in each language. An event
with no pin line or `cardLocation` falls back to its English postal
`location`.

`scripts/_event_cards.py` decides which pages have a card and what it says.
Three scripts read it, so they cannot disagree:

- `scripts/build-event-og.py` draws the cards, in the general card's style.
- `inject-seo.py` points each event page's `og:image` and `twitter:image` at
  its card, with alt text in the page's locale, and uses the card as the
  `image` of that event's JSON-LD `Event` node.
- `social-post.py` attaches the card to a news post that links to the page.

The cards are not committed (`assets/og/events/*.jpg` is gitignored).
`pages-deploy.yml` renders them on every deploy and refuses to publish if the
count differs from three per event page. `social-bluesky.yml` renders
them before posting, with `continue-on-error`, so a Chrome failure posts the
general card. A new event page, or a date moved by the Indico sync, reaches
the card on the next deploy with nothing to run by hand.

The cards carry no application deadline: a platform caches a preview for days,
and a passed deadline would stay on it. News posts are in English and take
the English card.

A title longer than 56 characters (`HEADING_MAX`) is cut at its first natural
break, a colon or a spaced dash, provided at least 12 characters stay: "Security
Beyond the State: Managing and Countering ..." becomes "Security Beyond the
State". With no break in reach, it is cut at the last whole word that fits and
ends with an ellipsis. The alt text keeps the full name. Behind that, the
card's CSS stops the title at two lines, the date at one and the venue at two,
so no text reaches the footer whatever the data holds. The venue wraps rather
than cuts, since the French and German institution names run longer, and the
city and country move to the second line together.

The cards are JPEGs at quality 88, about 70 KB each against 280 KB as PNG.
WhatsApp is widely reported to drop preview images over 300 KB.

Each page names its own locale's card as `<page>.jpg?v=<hash>` (or
`.fr.jpg`, `.de.jpg`). The hash covers what the
card shows and the builder's own source, so a moved date or a template change
gives the card a new URL and platforms fetch it again instead of serving their
cached copy for days. `inject-seo.py` writes the stamp, so an edit to
`build-event-og.py` needs `python3 scripts/inject-seo.py --seo-only`
afterwards, and the SEO check fails until it is run. The Indico sync already
runs it. `check-i18n-drift.py` ignores the stamp, since it changes in all
three locales at once.

```bash
python3 scripts/build-event-og.py              # every event page
python3 scripts/build-event-og.py --only essc-2027
```

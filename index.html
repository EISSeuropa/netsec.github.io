About

Overcoming fragmentation in European security studies

NetSec addresses the fragmentation of Europe's intellectual and analytical base in security studies. That fragmentation hinders the EU's pursuit of strategic autonomy and its ability to respond to complex security challenges.

The Action fosters synergies across national and local epistemic communities, bridges the academia–policy divide, and addresses gender and generational imbalances in the field. Through interdisciplinary research, annual conferences, policy workshops, a mentorship programme, and a summer school, NetSec contributes to a cohesive European security studies community.

By connecting academic, policy, and practitioner communities across the continent, NetSec advances Europe's strategic autonomy in an increasingly complex global security environment.

1

2

Closing the gap between research and decision-making on European security.

Developing Skills, Methods & Tools

Equipping the next generation of security scholars to address fragmentation.

Structure

Four Working Groups

The Action's three objectives are pursued through four interconnected Working Groups, each anchored in a distinct mission and contributing to the wider network.

WG1

Building the Network

Mapping scholars, institutions, and projects into a shared directory, and growing a diverse community across European security studies.

Lead Dr Moritz Weiss
Co-lead Prof Filip Ejdus
WG2

Transfer of Knowledge

Bridging research and policy through stakeholder workshops, policy briefs, and a quarterly newsletter for those who shape European security.

Lead Dr John Helferich
Co-lead Ms Alisa Kerschbaum
WG3

Fostering the Next Generation of Scholars

Backing early-career researchers with a summer school, mentoring, research awards, and mobility grants for short scientific missions.

Lead Dr Chiara Ruffa
Co-lead Dr Silvia D'Amato
WG4

Inclusion, Representativeness & Ethics

Monitoring diversity across regions, genders, and disciplines, funding inclusive participation, and upholding research ethics throughout.

Lead Dr Revecca Pedi
Co-lead Dr Šárka Kolmašová

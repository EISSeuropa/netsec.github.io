About

Overcoming fragmentation in European security studies

NetSec addresses the fragmentation of Europe's intellectual and analytical base in security studies — fragmentation that hinders the EU's pursuit of strategic autonomy and its ability to respond to complex security challenges.

The Action fosters synergies across national and local epistemic communities, bridges the academia–policy divide, and addresses gender and generational imbalances in the field. Through interdisciplinary research, annual conferences, policy workshops, a mentorship programme, and a summer school, NetSec contributes to a cohesive European security studies community.

By connecting academic, policy, and practitioner communities across the continent, NetSec advances Europe's strategic autonomy in an increasingly complex global security environment.

1

Inclusive Network for Knowledge Exchange

Building a multidisciplinary community across academia, policy, and practice.

2

Translating Knowledge into Policy Impact

Closing the gap between research and decision-making on European security.

3

Developing Skills, Methods & Tools

Equipping the next generation of security scholars to address fragmentation.

Four Working Groups

The Action's three objectives are pursued through four interconnected Working Groups, each anchored in a distinct mission and contributing to the wider network.

9–11 June 2026 Training School

NetSec Early-Career Scholars Summer School

Three days of training and mentoring for early-career researchers in European security — research presentations, individualised feedback from senior scholars, and structured mentorship. Open to PhD candidates and post-docs (within one year of defence) in strategic studies and international security, as well as SWAMOS / Euro-SWAMOS alumni. Travel and hotel reimbursement is available for 15 PhD students, and selected participants receive free access to the European Security Conference that follows.

Stockholm University · Frescativägen 114 18, Sweden
Scientific Coordinators: Dr Silvia D'Amato · Prof Chiara Ruffa
Application deadline: 1 March 2026 · Apply via ecs.netsec@eiss-europa.com
Full details & how to apply
11–12 June 2026 Annual Conference

European Security Conference

The flagship annual gathering of the NetSec community: an interdisciplinary conference on European defence and security open to academics, policy-makers, and practitioners. Held back-to-back with the Summer School so early-career scholars can join the wider conversation.

11 Jun 08:00 — 12 Jun 20:00 (Europe/Stockholm)
Stockholm University · Frescativägen, 114 19 Stockholm, Sweden
Chairpersons: Dr Julia Carver · Dr Sanne Verschuren

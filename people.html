The Directory

An open directory of researchers, policy-makers, and practitioners working in or alongside European security studies, Action leadership, Working Group participants, and a wider community. Anyone engaged with the field is welcome to add their bio.

👋 First time here?

This is the NetSec open directory. Anyone working in or alongside European security studies is welcome, not only Management Committee members.

  • 🔍 Search by name, affiliation, or country.
  • 🎚️ Filter by working group, Management Committee role, or mentorship status.
  • 🏷️ Drill in by research interest: tap a chip above the directory, or any keyword pill on a member card, to narrow the results.
  • 🤝 Mentorship: members opt in as Available to mentor or Seeking mentorship. Filter to either and a matching panel pairs offers with requests. Narrow it by research theme to find someone in your research area.
  • 🪪 Switch density in the toolbar to fit more on screen.
  • 👤 Anyone can join, see the form at the foot of the page.
